# Scheduling & Booking

## How to book

You can book a course in three ways:

1. Online through the booking page on our website.
2. By phone with our bookings team.
3. By email — send us the course you want and your preferred dates and we will confirm availability.

See the "Contact" topic for our phone number and email address.

To secure your place you pay either the full course fee or a £79 deposit. Places are confirmed on a first-come, first-served basis.

## When courses run

We run courses on a rolling schedule throughout the year.

- **Weekday courses** run Monday to Friday at our Manchester centre.
- **Evening courses** for selected modules run on weekday evenings at our Manchester centre.
- **Weekend courses** run on Saturdays and Sundays at our Leeds venue.

Yes — we do offer weekend classes. Weekend courses are popular and tend to fill up quickly, so we recommend booking early.

## Multi-day courses

For multi-day courses such as the Door Supervisor course, all days must be completed. Weekday courses run on consecutive days; weekend courses run across consecutive weekends where needed.

## Rescheduling

- You can reschedule to a later course free of charge if you give us at least 7 days' notice before your start date.
- Rescheduling with less than 7 days' notice may incur a £40 administration fee.
- See the "Refunds" topic for our cancellation and refund policy.

## Start times

Courses start at 9:00 am and usually finish by 5:00 pm. Please arrive 15 minutes early on the first day to complete registration.
