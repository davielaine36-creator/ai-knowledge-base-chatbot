# Refunds & Cancellations

We want you to book with confidence. Our refund policy depends on how much notice you give before your course start date.

## Cancellation by you

- **More than 14 days' notice:** full refund of any fees paid, minus a £25 administration fee.
- **7 to 14 days' notice:** 50% refund of the course fee.
- **Less than 7 days' notice:** no refund, but you may transfer to a future course once (a £40 transfer fee applies).

Deposits (£79) are non-refundable, but they can be applied to a rescheduled course if you give at least 7 days' notice.

## Cancellation by Brown Academy

If we cancel or reschedule a course for any reason, you are entitled to a full refund of all fees paid, or you can transfer to another date at no extra cost.

## Failed assessments

If you do not pass your end-of-course assessment, you are entitled to one free re-sit within 30 days. The course fee is not refundable on the basis of a failed assessment.

## Missed course days

If you do not attend a course day without rescheduling in advance, this is treated as a late cancellation and no refund is due for that course.

## How to request a refund

Email our support team with your booking reference and the reason for cancellation. Approved refunds are processed back to your original payment method within 10 working days.
