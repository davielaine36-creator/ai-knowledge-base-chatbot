# Pricing

All prices below are in GBP and include course materials and certification fees, unless stated otherwise. Prices do not include the separate licence application fee paid to the licensing authority.

## New entrant courses

- Door Supervisor Course: £329
- Security Guarding Course: £229
- CCTV (Public Space Surveillance) Course: £209
- Emergency First Aid at Work (EFAW): £89
- Close Protection Foundation: £249

## Requalification (top-up) courses

- Door Supervisor Requalification: £159
- Security Guarding Requalification: £99

## Payment options

- Pay in full at the time of booking by debit or credit card.
- Split payment: pay a £79 deposit to secure your place, with the balance due no later than 7 days before the course start date.

## Discounts

- Group bookings of 4 or more learners: 10% off each place.
- Returning students who previously trained with us: £20 off any requalification course.

## What's included

- All course materials and workbooks.
- The end-of-course assessment.
- One free re-sit of any failed assessment within 30 days.

## What's not included

- The licence application fee, which is paid directly to the licensing authority.
- Travel and accommodation.
