# Entry Requirements & What to Bring

## Who can enrol

- You must be at least 18 years old on or before the first day of the course.
- You must have a basic level of English (reading, writing, listening, and speaking) to take part in assessments.
- For licence-linked courses, you will later need to pass a criminal record check as part of your licence application. Brown Academy does not carry out this check, but our team can explain the process.

## What you need to bring on the day

Please bring the following to every course day:

1. **Photographic ID** — a valid passport or UK driving licence. This is required to confirm your identity for assessments.
2. **Proof of National Insurance number** — for example a payslip, a letter from HMRC, or your National Insurance card.
3. A **pen and notepad** for taking notes.
4. **Lunch** or money for lunch (there are cafés near both venues).

## What to wear

- Wear comfortable clothing and flat shoes. The Door Supervisor and requalification courses include physical intervention training, so you will be moving around.

## Documents we provide

- All workbooks, handouts, and learning materials are provided on the day. You do not need to buy any textbooks in advance.

## Accessibility

If you have a disability, learning need, or require any reasonable adjustment, contact our support team before your course start date so we can make arrangements.
