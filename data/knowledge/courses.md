# Courses

Brown Academy offers the following accredited training courses.

## Door Supervisor Course

Our most popular course, for anyone who wants to work on the door at pubs, clubs, events, and venues. It covers working in the private security industry, conflict management, physical intervention, and first aid.

- Duration: 6 days (in person).
- Outcome: qualification required to apply for a Door Supervisor licence.
- Class size: maximum 16 learners.

## Security Guarding Course

For learners who want to work as a static or mobile security guard protecting premises and property.

- Duration: 4 days (in person).
- Outcome: qualification required to apply for a Security Guarding licence.

## CCTV (Public Space Surveillance) Course

For learners who want to work as a CCTV operator monitoring public spaces.

- Duration: 3 days (in person).
- Outcome: qualification required to apply for a CCTV operator licence.

## Emergency First Aid at Work (EFAW)

A 1-day certified first aid course. It is included in the Door Supervisor course, but can also be taken on its own.

- Duration: 1 day.
- Certificate valid for: 3 years.

## Close Protection Foundation

An introductory course for learners interested in close protection (bodyguard) work. This is a foundation course and does not by itself qualify you for a Close Protection licence.

- Duration: 2 days.

## Requalification (Top-Up) Training

Short top-up courses for existing licence holders who need to renew. See the "Requalification" topic for full details.
