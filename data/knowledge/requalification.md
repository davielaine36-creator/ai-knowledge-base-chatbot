# Requalification (Top-Up Training)

Requalification, also called "top-up" training, lets existing licence holders renew their licence by completing updated training. This is required when your security licence is approaching its expiry date.

## Who needs requalification?

If you already hold a Door Supervisor or Security Guarding licence and want to renew it, you must complete the relevant top-up training before you reapply for the licence.

## Door Supervisor Requalification

- Duration: 2 days (in person).
- Covers refreshed conflict management and physical intervention training, plus updated legislation.
- Outcome: the qualification needed to renew a Door Supervisor licence.

## Security Guarding Requalification

- Duration: 1 day (in person).
- Covers refreshed knowledge and updated legislation.
- Outcome: the qualification needed to renew a Security Guarding licence.

## When should I book?

We recommend completing your requalification at least 8 weeks before your current licence expires, to allow time for your renewal application to be processed.

## What if my licence has already expired?

You can still take requalification training if your licence has recently expired. If it has been expired for a long time, contact our support team and we will advise whether requalification or a full new course is the best route for you.
