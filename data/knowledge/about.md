# About Brown Academy

Brown Academy is a professional security training and requalification academy. We help people start, maintain, and advance careers in the private security industry through accredited, hands-on courses.

We have trained over 4,000 security professionals since 2014. Our trainers are former door supervisors, close protection officers, and control room operators with real-world experience.

## What we do

- Deliver accredited training courses for new entrants to the security industry.
- Run requalification (top-up) training so existing licence holders can renew.
- Provide first aid, conflict management, and physical intervention training.
- Offer guidance on applying for and renewing industry licences.

## Where we are

Our main training centre is in central Manchester, with additional weekend sessions delivered at our Leeds venue. All courses are delivered in person at one of our venues — we do not currently offer fully online training.

## Why students choose us

- Small class sizes (maximum 16 learners per course).
- Flexible weekday, evening, and weekend scheduling.
- Free re-sit of any failed assessment within 30 days.
- A dedicated student support team to help with licence applications.
