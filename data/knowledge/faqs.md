# Frequently Asked Questions

## Do you offer weekend classes?

Yes. We run weekend courses on Saturdays and Sundays at our Leeds venue. Weekend places are popular and fill up quickly, so we recommend booking early.

## Do I need any experience to start?

No prior experience is needed for our new entrant courses, such as the Door Supervisor and Security Guarding courses. They are designed for people starting in the industry.

## How long does the Door Supervisor course take?

The Door Supervisor course runs over 6 days in person.

## Will I get my licence at the end of the course?

No. The course gives you the qualification you need to then apply for your licence with the licensing authority. The licence application and its fee are separate from your course.

## How much does requalification cost?

Door Supervisor requalification is £159 and Security Guarding requalification is £99. See the "Pricing" topic for full details.

## What do I need to bring on the day?

Bring photographic ID (passport or UK driving licence), proof of your National Insurance number, a pen and notepad, and lunch. See the "Requirements" topic for full details.

## Can I pay in instalments?

You can secure your place with a £79 deposit and pay the balance no later than 7 days before the course starts.

## Can I get a refund if I cancel?

Yes, depending on notice. More than 14 days' notice gets a full refund minus a £25 administration fee; 7–14 days gets a 50% refund; less than 7 days is non-refundable but you can reschedule (a £40 reschedule fee applies). See the "Refunds" topic for full details.

## Do you offer online courses?

No. All of our courses are delivered in person at our Manchester or Leeds venues.

## How old do I need to be?

You must be at least 18 years old on or before the first day of the course.

## How do I schedule a course?

You can book online, by phone, or by email. See the "Scheduling" and "Contact" topics for details.
